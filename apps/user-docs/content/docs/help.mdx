## Common questions

**Why did my test not run?** Only approved versions run. After an edit the version is *Needs review*. See [Reviewing AI tests](/docs/test-cases/reviewing-ai-tests).

**Why did a click hit the wrong element?** Click targets match non-strictly and take the first match. See [how click targets resolve](/docs/reference/test-steps#how-click-targets-resolve).

**Why does an assertion fail although the text appears later?** `contains`, `equals`, and `urlMatches` do not wait. Add a **waitFor** step first.

**Why did CI not start a run?** Work through [CI troubleshooting](/docs/ci-cd/troubleshooting).

**Why can't I start a run from the dashboard?** Your organization is at its concurrent-run limit, or billing blocks runs. See [Running tests](/docs/executions/running-tests).

**What leaves my repository?** See [Security and data handling](/docs/security).

## Contact support

Use **Need help?** at the bottom right of the ProoflineQA website. Provide a title, a description, your email, and optionally a screenshot (image, up to 5 MB). Include your **Organization id** from [Account settings](/docs/settings/account).

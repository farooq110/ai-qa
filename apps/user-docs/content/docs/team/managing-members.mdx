The **Team** page lists members with role and join date. Non-admins see it read-only.

## Invite a member

**Invite member** (Admin) → name, work email, role → **Send invite**. A **temporary password** is generated and shown **once**; copy it and share it through a secure channel. The member should change it under **Settings → Personal**.

## Change a role or remove a member

Use the role dropdown on a member's row; it applies immediately. The trash icon removes access immediately (you cannot remove yourself). Projects, tests, and run history stay with the organization.

What each role can do: [Roles and permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions). To change it: [Customizing roles](/docs/team/customizing-roles).

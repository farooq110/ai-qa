---
title: Customizing Roles
description: Choose which modules and actions QA and Developer may use.
---

Open **Roles** in the sidebar (or **Manage roles** on the Team page). Each module (Overview, Projects, Test cases, Executions, CI / CD, Team, Billing, Settings, Roles) has a checkbox per action: view, create, edit, delete, approve, run, or manage.

1. Pick the **QA** or **Developer** tab.
2. Tick the actions to allow.
3. **Save changes**. They apply on the member's next request.

Rules:

- **Admins always have full access** and cannot be edited.
- Unticking **view** clears the module's other actions; a member who cannot see a module cannot act in it.
- **Reset to defaults** restores the built-in permissions for that role (see [Roles and permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions)).
- Members without the manage-roles permission see the page read-only.

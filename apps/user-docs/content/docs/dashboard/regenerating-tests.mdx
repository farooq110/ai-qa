---
title: Regenerating Tests
description: Add new candidates after your application changes.
---

Regeneration adds new **Pending review** candidates to your library. It never edits, removes, or re-opens approved or manual tests.

Use it after you re-run [analysis](/docs/dashboard/analyzing-your-repository) on a changed branch:

1. Open **Overview** and click **Regenerate**.
2. For a wider pass, open the arrow menu and pick **Full regeneration**; confirm the dialog.
3. Review the new candidates under **Test cases**.

Duplicates are skipped by title, so repeating a regeneration does not flood the queue.

<Callout type="warn">
  Each regeneration spends AI tokens from the monthly pool. Watch the **AI N%** chip in the top bar.
</Callout>

---
title: Managing Your Subscription
description: Change card or plan, cancel, and resume.
---

Only **Admins** can change billing. Other roles see the plan read-only.

## After subscribing

- **Manage billing** opens the external billing portal: update the card, view invoices, change plan or interval.
- The usage meter is described in [Usage and quotas](/docs/billing/usage-and-quotas).

## Cancel and resume

**Cancel plan** → optional reason → **Cancel at period end**. You keep full access until the period end date; the page then shows *Cancels* with a **Resume subscription** button. After the period ends, generation and runs pause and you pick a plan again. Your projects, tests, and history are kept.

## Failed payments

A failed payment shows a badge on Overview. Resolve it in **Manage billing**. See [Billing statuses](/docs/billing/billing-statuses) for what pauses and when.

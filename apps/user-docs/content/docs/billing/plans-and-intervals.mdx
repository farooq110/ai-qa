Billing is per organization. A plan sets three limits:

| Limit | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **AI tokens / month** | Pool used by generation and AI drafts. |
| **Concurrent runs** | How many runs may be queued or running at once. |
| **Projects** | How many repositories you can import. |

The **Billing** page always shows current limits and prices; this page does not repeat them so they cannot go stale.

## Start a plan

1. Choose an interval: **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly**. Longer intervals show the effective monthly rate.
2. Click **Start trial** (first plan) or **Subscribe** on a plan card.
3. Complete checkout. For a trial you add a card and are **not charged until it ends**; the Billing page shows the end date.

Until a plan is active you can browse, but importing projects, generating, and running open a *Start a plan* prompt (Admins) or suggest asking an Admin.

Every plan includes the approval gate, GitHub, Bitbucket, and GitLab connections, CI integrations, run history with evidence, and the three roles.

Changes, cancellation, and invoices: [Managing your subscription](/docs/billing/managing-your-subscription).

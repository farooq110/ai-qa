## Sign in and sessions

Sign in with your work email and password. Sessions expire after **8 hours**; you are asked to sign in again. If you have not confirmed your email, the sign-in page offers **Resend the email**.

## Reset a password

1. Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email.
2. Open the emailed link within **1 hour**.
3. Set a new password (at least 12 characters).

The confirmation screen is the same whether or not the address has an account, so it does not reveal which emails are registered.

To change your password while signed in, use **Settings → Personal → Password**. See [Account settings](/docs/settings/account).

## Theme

Dark is the default. Switch with the toggle at the bottom of the sidebar, or choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **System** under **Settings → Personal → Appearance**. The choice is stored in your browser.

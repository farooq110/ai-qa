An account belongs to an **organization** — the workspace that holds projects, tests, members, and the subscription. The person who creates it is its first **Admin**.

## Sign up

Click **Start free** on the website and complete **Create your organization**.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Full name, Work email, Organization name | Required. |
| Password | At least 12 characters. |
| Industry, Organization size, Country / region | Optional. |

A confirmation link is emailed to you. It expires after **24 hours**. **Resend the email** is available after a 30-second wait; if you mistyped the address, use **Wrong email? Start over**.

After you confirm, you go straight to the plan list — see [Plans and billing](/docs/billing/plans-and-intervals).

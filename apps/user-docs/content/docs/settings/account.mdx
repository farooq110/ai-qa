## Organization tab

A read-only profile (name, industry, your role) and an **Organization id** to quote to support. Repository providers and environments are further down; see [Repository providers](/docs/settings/repository-providers) and [Environments](/docs/settings/environments). Plan, usage, and invoices live on [Billing](/docs/billing/plans-and-intervals).

## Personal tab

| Section | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Full name (up to 80 characters) is shown to teammates in reviews and the audit trail. Email is read-only; contact support to change it. |
| Password | Current password plus a new one of at least 12 characters. You stay signed in and receive a confirmation email. |
| Appearance | **Dark** (default), **Light**, or **System**. Stored on this device. |
| Session | Access tokens expire after 8 hours. |
